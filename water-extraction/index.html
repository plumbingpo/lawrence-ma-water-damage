Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Lawrence Restoration Squad provides emergency water extraction throughout Lawrence using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Why Water Extraction Is Not a DIY Job

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
