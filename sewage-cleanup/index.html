Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Lawrence Restoration Squad provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Lawrence, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Lawrence

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
