When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Lawrence

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
  • The subfloor gives or feels spongy underfoot where water once stood.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • In case the insurance company asks down the road, photograph anything damaged while it is still in place.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Ventilate with the bathroom exhaust fan while showering and afterward to protect new drywall.
  • Completed-repair photos and the scope document belong in your home records.
